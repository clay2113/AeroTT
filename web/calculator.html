How it works

CdA estimate

CdA describes how much aerodynamic drag your rider and bike create. In this app, the camera estimates frontal area from the silhouette and applies the selected drag coefficient.

FormulaCdA = frontal area (A) x drag coefficient (Cd)

The camera-derived value is an estimate. Position, clothing, wind, and calibration quality all affect it.
